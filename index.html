TACTICAL BRIEFING

RULES OF ENGAGEMENT & FAQ

Essential protocols for competing operatives during the 24-hour sprint.

What are the rules regarding pre-existing code and repositories? ▼

All core application code must be written during the 24-hour hackathon window after HOD activation. You may utilize open-source libraries, pretrained model weights, and public frameworks, provided they are cited in your final submission dossier.

Who is eligible to participate in CODEAPEX 3.0? ▼

Students currently enrolled in any accredited undergraduate, postgraduate, or diploma engineering program are eligible. Cross-college and cross-specialization teams (1 to 4 members) are allowed.

What accommodations are provided during the 24-hour offline Grand Finale? ▼

High-speed Wi-Fi, continuous power stations, round-the-clock refreshments, meals, midnight snacks, security escorts, and dedicated rest areas are provided at the VIT Bibwewadi Campus.

What should teams bring to the offline venue? ▼

Operatives must bring valid college identification cards, laptops, chargers, extension cords, any required hardware boards (e.g. Raspberry Pi, Arduino for Track 03), and personal toiletries.
